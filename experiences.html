HOW WE MEET

Three Distinct Formats

Each experience is thoughtfully designed to prioritize authentic human connection over passive attendance.

01 SALON

Design Conversations

Closed-door roundtables exploring architectural typologies, zoning evolutions, and emerging vernacular techniques. Unfiltered perspectives among studio principals.

02 LAB

Material Explorations

Hands-on sensory workshops. Designers and master fabricators examine natural Italian marble slabs, fluted walnut joinery, hand-cast brass hardware, and acoustic textiles.

03 STUDIO

Studio Charrettes

Intimate visits to notable studios and active construction sites. Experience spatial journeys guided directly by the project’s principal architects.
