01 / GENESIS

Why A’bcd Exists

“Good design is a conversation. Not a monologue, and not a transaction.”

Every day across studios, construction sites, and material workshops, architects and interior designers make thousands of consequential decisions. Yet too often, our profession operates in silos—divided by studios, cities, and disciplines.

A’bcd was conceived to change that. Initiated by AAREN INTPRO, this platform is not a corporate billboard or a product catalog. It is an independent, intellectual sanctuary designed to give the creative community a place to assemble, share real execution hurdles, and discover collaborative possibilities beyond the drawing board.

We celebrate the creators—the architects who imagine spaces, the interior designers who breathe warmth into volume, and the material pioneers who continually push the boundaries of texture, light, and durability.

OUR FOUNDATION

A Different Way to Connect

Rather than inflating arbitrary metrics, A’bcd is defined by three unwavering values.

01

People over Profiles

We believe meaningful relationships begin with genuine conversations, shared interests, and a willingness to learn from one another. We leave titles and sales agendas at the door.

  • Direct studio-to-studio dialogues
  • Peer mentorship & honest critiques
  • Transparent knowledge sharing
02

Design beyond Disciplines

Architecture, interiors, materials, lighting, furniture, and technology intersect. We bring different perspectives into the same conversation to spark richer holistic solutions.

  • Cross-pollination across AEC sectors
  • Material science & craftsmanship
  • Sustainability & regional techniques
03

Community beyond Business

Not every interaction needs to become a transaction. We want to create a space where relationships grow naturally, camaraderie develops, and enduring opportunities follow.

  • No commercial solicitation pressure
  • Curated gatherings with authentic hospitality
  • A collective that elevates the entire fraternity

COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Terms & Community Code

A Community Initiative by AAREN INTPRO • Established 2026

1. The Spirit of A’bcd

A’bcd is dedicated to bringing architects, interior designers, and the wider design fraternity together to exchange ideas, discover possibilities, and build relationships beyond the drawing board. Participation is predicated on mutual professional respect and creative generosity.

2. Community Values: People over Profiles

Members agree to engage in constructive dialogue. We prioritize genuine conversations over transactions, cross-disciplinary exchange over silos, and long-term relationships over aggressive commercial solicitation.
