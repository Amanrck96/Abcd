COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Terms & Community Code

A Community Initiative by AAREN INTPRO • Established 2026

1. The Spirit of A’bcd

A’bcd is dedicated to bringing architects, interior designers, and the wider design fraternity together to exchange ideas, discover possibilities, and build relationships beyond the drawing board. Participation is predicated on mutual professional respect and creative generosity.

2. Community Values: People over Profiles

Members agree to engage in constructive dialogue. We prioritize genuine conversations over transactions, cross-disciplinary exchange over silos, and long-term relationships over aggressive commercial solicitation.

3. Curated Gatherings & Shared Materials

Participation in A’bcd experiences, design dialogues, and material library sessions is hosted under the stewardship of Aaren Intpro. Intellectual property shared in studio sessions remains the property of respective creators.

4. Integrity of Practice

We uphold the highest ethical standards of architectural practice, design authorship, and environmental responsibility across the built environment.

DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026 • Aaren Intpro Initiative

1. Information We Collect

When you register for A’bcd membership or attend an experience, we collect professional contact details (name, email, practice/studio, city, discipline). This ensures our community remains tailored to practitioners of architecture and interior design.

2. Respect for Your Privacy

We will never sell or rent your personal contact information to third-party data brokers or commercial advertisers. Your credentials are used exclusively for A’bcd community invitations, curated gatherings, and platform communications.

3. Security & Governance

Under the stewardship of Aaren Intpro, industry-grade security protocols protect member data at all times.
