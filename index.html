03 / Expeditions & Field Studies

Architectural Travel

Immersive architectural travel expeditions, global design tours, heritage study trips, and city walks curated specifically for architects and creative practitioners.

5 Days • 12 Places
Kyoto & Kansai, Japan

Modernist Timber & Zen Spatial Geometry

Study traditional sukiya-zukuri carpentry alongside contemporary timber masterworks by Tadao Ando, Kengo Kuma, and SANAA. Private temple access and architect-guided tours.

Curator: Kenzo Takahashi (Architect & Historian)
Group: Strictly limited to 12 practitioners
4 Days • 14 Places
Copenhagen, Denmark

Nordic Urbanism & Sustainable Civic Living

An insider exploration of Danish sustainable urbanism, circular timber construction, waterfront revitalization, and adaptive reuse architecture across Copenhagen and Malmö.

Curator: Astrid Møller (Urban Architect)
Group: Strictly limited to 14 practitioners
4 Days • 10 Places
Vals & Basel, Switzerland

Swiss Stone Architecture & Thermal Sanctuaries

Experience Peter Zumthor's Therme Vals, Herzog & de Meuron's Vitra Campus, and private Swiss architectural studios focused on monolithic craftsmanship and local stone materiality.

Curator: Christian Weiss (Spatial Designer)
Group: Strictly limited to 10 practitioners
